Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Stenora is designed around a single principle: your voice stays on your device.

  • Stenora does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in. No analytics, ads, or third-party trackers.
  • Recordings, transcripts, speaker names, and summaries are stored on your device, and all processing (recording, transcription, speaker identification, AI cleanup and summary) happens on-device.
  • Optional iCloud Backup. If you turn on “Back up recordings to iCloud” in Settings (off by default), your recordings are included in your device’s standard, encrypted iCloud backup — stored in your own iCloud account, never sent to us or anyone else. Your speaker profiles (voice data) are always excluded from backup, even when this is on.
  • Permissions. Microphone (only while recording) and Speech Recognition (Apple’s on-device engine — no audio is sent to Apple or anyone). You can revoke either anytime in iOS Settings.
  • The one network request. An optional, one-time HTTPS download of an AI model file from a public model host (Hugging Face) for higher-quality summaries. It downloads a file and sends no audio, transcript, or personal data — an anonymous fetch. Skip it and Stenora uses Apple’s built-in on-device model, fully offline.
  • Data collection: none under Apple’s definitions.
  • TestFlight betas only. Apple (not Stenora) automatically collects crash and usage data from your test session and shares it with the developer; this cannot be disabled in-app and applies only to the beta. The App Store version has no such collection.
  • Recording others. Record only with the consent of everyone involved; laws vary by location.
