Stenora

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email stefanhaotian@gmail.com. Stenora is made by one independent developer, and every message is read.

  • Supported iPhones. Stenora needs iOS 26 or later. On iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and every iPhone 16 and 17 model, titles and summaries work with Apple Intelligence, or after a one-time model download over Wi-Fi. On iPhone 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max, 13 Pro, 13 Pro Max, every iPhone 14 model, iPhone 15 and iPhone 15 Plus, summaries need that one-time download. Other iPhones that run iOS 26 transcribe, label speakers, search and play back, without AI cleanup, titles or summaries.
  • Languages. Stenora detects the language and transcribes Chinese (Mandarin), English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. Each recording is processed in one main language, and its summary is written in that language.
  • Free recordings and the unlock. Recording and importing are always unlimited. Your first 3 recordings are fully processed for free; a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited processing. There is no subscription.
  • Restore a purchase. Open Settings in Stenora and tap Restore Purchases in the Unlimited processing section, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.
  • Where your recordings live. Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them, unless you turned on “Include recordings in iCloud Backup” in Settings, which is off by default.
  • Privacy. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is collected. Read the privacy policy.

技术支持

  • 联系方式。有问题、遇到故障或有建议，请发邮件至 stefanhaotian@gmail.com。溯录由一位独立开发者制作，每封邮件都会读。
  • 支持的设备。需要 iOS 26 或更高版本。iPhone 15 Pro、15 Pro Max 以及 iPhone 16、17 全系列：通过 Apple 智能，或通过 Wi-Fi 下载一次本机模型后，生成标题和摘要。iPhone 12 Pro、12 Pro Max、13 Pro、13 Pro Max、iPhone 14 全系列、iPhone 15、15 Plus：下载一次本机模型后生成摘要。其他可运行 iOS 26 的 iPhone：可以转文字、区分说话人、搜索和回放，暂不支持 AI 整理、标题和摘要。
  • 语言。自动识别语言，支持中文（普通话）、英语、日语、韩语、德语、法语、西班牙语、意大利语、葡萄牙语转文字。每段录音按一种主要语言处理，摘要也用这种语言写成。
  • 免费额度与买断。录音和导入始终不受限制。前 3 段录音免费完整处理；一次买断即可无限处理，不订阅。
  • 恢复购买。在溯录中打开“设置”，在“无限处理”部分点“恢复购买”，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户登录。
  • 录音存在哪里。只存在你的 iPhone 上。删除 App 会同时删除录音，除非你在“设置”中打开了“将录音包含在 iCloud 备份中”（默认关闭）。
  • 隐私。不上传，不收集任何数据。详见隐私政策。
